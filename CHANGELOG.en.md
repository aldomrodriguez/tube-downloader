## [1.0.1] — 2026-11-20

- yt-dlp updated to 2026.10.26 to keep up with YouTube changes.
- Builds now always use the latest version of yt-dlp.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-20

First public release.

- Downloads in the highest quality available, 4K included.
- Delivers MP4 in H.264 or H.265, ready to edit in Premiere Pro.
- Converts only when needed, on the graphics card (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) with automatic
  fallback to the CPU.
- Full playlist downloads.
- Real progress with speed, time remaining and a stop button.
- Auto-update: tells you when a new version is out and installs it by itself.
