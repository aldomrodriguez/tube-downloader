# Code of conduct

[🇦🇷 Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · 🇬🇧 **English**

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Treat others with respect and kindness, including when we disagree.
- Give and receive constructive criticism about the code, not about people.
- Be patient with people who are just starting out: we were all beginners once.
- Own up to our mistakes and learn from them.

## What isn't accepted

- Insults, derogatory comments or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience conduct that doesn't respect this code, write to the
maintainer through [aldorodriguez.com.ar](https://aldorodriguez.com.ar). All reports are
reviewed and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that
don't respect this code, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.
